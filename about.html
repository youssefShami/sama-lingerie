A little yellow boutique in Saida.

Hello from Saida.

Sama started with a simple observation: the pieces women actually live in were either overpriced or an afterthought in Saida's shops.

So in 2025 we opened a small yellow boutique on Ghassan Hammoud Street and filled it with everyday lingerie and loungewear imported from Turkey.
